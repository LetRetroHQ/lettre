# Welcome to Lettre

Welcome to Lettre Journal, a sample publication built with Lettre by LetRetro. The posts, subscribers, and delivery history here are demo data, ready for you to explore.

![The Lettre mark](lettre-logo.png)

Write in Markdown, preview your email, and schedule it for your readers. Every issue can also have a home in your archive.

Open the dashboard to try a draft, explore your subscriber list, or review a send.
